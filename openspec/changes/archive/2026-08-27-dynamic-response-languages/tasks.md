### Notes on deviations from the original checklist

- Task 10.1c originally said not to add a session-override field to `Settings` at all (correct) but didn't name the eventual provider; it landed as `activeAsrModelOverrideProvider` in `recording_provider.dart` rather than a new file, since it's small and only that file's notifier needs to react to it.
